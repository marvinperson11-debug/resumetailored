Tailoring versus building

A builder helps you structure a resume once. Tailoring adapts it for each application, so the words in your resume line up with the words in the posting, which is what keyword-based ATS screening looks at.

ResumeTailored keeps every claim truthful: it reframes and rephrases your real experience in each job's language and never invents skills or roles.
