Cancellation FAQs

Do I get a refund if I cancel mid-month?▾
We don't offer prorated refunds, but you keep full Pro access through the end of your current billing period. So if you paid for the month, you use the month.
How fast does cancellation take effect?▾
We process all cancellation requests within 1 hour. You'll receive a confirmation email once it's done. Your Pro access continues until your billing period ends.
Can I pause instead of cancel?▾
We don't currently offer a pause feature, but canceling and re-subscribing later is just as easy. You'll always get the same $19.99/month rate — we don't price-lock or penalize returning members.
What if I was charged but didn't mean to subscribe?▾
Contact us within 7 days of the charge and we'll issue a full refund, no questions asked. We never want anyone paying for something they didn't intend to buy.
Will my resume history be deleted if I cancel?▾
No. Your career check-in data and community posts remain intact. If you re-subscribe later, everything is right where you left it.
