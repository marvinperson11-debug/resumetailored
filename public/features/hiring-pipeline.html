Most job boards stop at "here's your applicant list." ResumeTailored's hiring pipeline is the part after that: a drag-and-drop board where every applicant lives as a card you move through your own process, instead of a spreadsheet you maintain by hand or an inbox you lose track of.

How it works

Four stages — New, Reviewed, Interview, Hired — cover most hiring processes out of the box. Drag a candidate's card to advance them, leave a note for your team, message them directly from the card, or send a one-click reject email that doesn't leave anyone hanging. It's paired with AI candidate matching, so the strongest applicants already sit near the top of New before you touch anything.

A lightweight ATS, not a project

There's no implementation project, no field mapping, no training session. Post a job, applicants land in New automatically, and the board is ready. It's built for the hiring volume small teams actually have — a handful of open roles, not thousands of requisitions.

What is the ResumeTailored hiring pipeline?
A drag-and-drop applicant tracking board: move each candidate through New, Reviewed, Interview, and Hired, with in-app messaging and one-click reject emails.
Is it a real applicant tracking system (ATS)?
Yes — a lightweight ATS for small hiring teams: pipeline stages, notes, messaging, and analytics, without enterprise setup overhead.
Can I message candidates without leaving the pipeline?
Yes. Every candidate card opens with an in-app message thread, so scheduling and rejections happen without switching to email.
