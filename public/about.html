About ResumeTailored

ResumeTailored helps job seekers get past the first screen, and helps small businesses hire without the busywork. One tool tailors your resume and cover letter to each job you apply for. The same platform lets employers post a role, manage candidates, and move a hire from first message to signed offer.

For job seekers: paste a job posting and your resume, and get a version rewritten for that exact role, with the keywords hiring software looks for. Every plan includes ATS scans, so you can see how your resume lines up before you send it. Free to start, no credit card required. Pro is $19 a month or $129 once, for life.

For employers: post a job, manage your pipeline, schedule interviews, send offers, and collect e-signatures in one place. Plans start free and scale to $299 a month for larger teams, with texting and background checks on paid plans.

Our standard: we say exactly what each plan includes, and we keep the pricing page as the source of truth. Any sample resumes, profiles, or dashboards you see on this site are illustrative examples, not real customers.
