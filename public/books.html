Frequently asked questions

Does Books handle my taxes for me?

No. Books is bookkeeping only. It generates tax-ready reports (Schedule C, IRS categories, quarterly estimates) that you can export to TurboTax, H&R Block, or your CPA.

Does connecting my bank move real money?

No. Bank feeds (via Plaid) let you see your transactions inside Books. Invoicing works the same way: you create and send invoices and mark them paid, but Books itself never moves money.

Where do I find Books?

Books lives inside your employer portal at /employer/books. Basic Books is included on the Portal plan and up.

Can Taylor make entries or send invoices for me?

Taylor can answer read-only questions about your Books data. It doesn't add entries, import a CSV, or send an invoice on its own.
