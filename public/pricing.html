Is the free plan really free?

Yes. Resume tailoring, cover letters and ATS scans have no daily cap — you just need a free account. Free exports carry a small watermark and use the 6 free templates.

What’s the difference between Pro monthly and Lifetime?

Both unlock every Pro tool. Lifetime is a one-time $129 payment and includes 30 tailoring-variant sets per calendar month; monthly Pro ($19/mo) has no variant cap.

Can I cancel?

Monthly plans cancel anytime from your account. Lifetime is a one-time purchase.
