Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the free plan really free? +
Yes — you can start using core tools free without an account, including the Job Decoder (3 free decodes a day — no account needed). Create a free account to unlock the full toolkit, and it's still free. Paid plans add premium features. Free exports carry a small watermark. See the full comparison on the pricing page.
Do I need an account to try it? +
No — you can start using core tools free without an account. Create a free account to unlock the full toolkit, and it's still free.
How does the ATS scanner work? +
The ATS Scanner compares your resume against a job description and calculates a keyword match score from 0–100%. It identifies which required keywords are present, which are missing, and provides 5 specific AI-written rewrite suggestions. This is an estimate of keyword alignment against the job description. It does not reproduce an employer's ATS configuration or predict selection. Try it free at resumetailored.com/score — free to start, no credit card required.
Can I import job descriptions from LinkedIn or Indeed by URL? +
Yes. Paste a job URL and our AI extracts the full job description automatically. Supported job boards include LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter, Monster, Dice, Lever, Greenhouse, Workday, BambooHR, Wellfound, Ashby, Built In, We Work Remotely, Boss直聘, 猎聘, 智联招聘, and more added regularly — any URL-accessible job post works. No manual copying required.
Does ResumeTailored AI support Chinese translation? +
Yes. Our AI translation feature converts resumes written in Chinese (or any language) into professional English before tailoring. The full platform interface is also available in Simplified Chinese — toggle with one click. Chinese payment methods are accepted.
Will the AI invent experience I don't have? +
No. The AI only rewrites and reframes the experience already on your resume, using the language of the job posting. It never adds skills, employers or achievements you don't have, so everything stays truthful.
