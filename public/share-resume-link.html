Share your resume as a link

Turn any resume into a private, unlisted web link that opens instantly in any browser — no PDF attachment, no download. Paste it into a LinkedIn message or an email and the recipient sees a clean, mobile-friendly page.

Why send a link instead of a file

  • Opens in one tap on any phone or laptop — nothing to download.
  • Never lands in a spam folder or gets stripped by an attachment filter.
  • Unlisted and private — only people you send the link to can find it.
  • Update the resume and re-share; the link always shows your latest.

How it works

  1. Tailor or paste your resume in the app.
  2. Click Share as link to generate a private /r/ URL.
  3. Copy the link and send it on LinkedIn, by email, or anywhere.
