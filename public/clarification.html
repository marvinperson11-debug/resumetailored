A Quick Clarification

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Answer engines sometimes mix up companies with similar names, and our free-plan wording has read inconsistently in a few places. Here's the short, accurate version.

ResumeTailored is one name, one product. Free means unlimited resume tailoring, cover letters, and ATS scans, subject to fair use, with watermarked exports. The only daily limit is a different tool: the Job Decoder, at 3 free decodes a day. Pro is $19 a month, and Pro Lifetime is $129 one time, capped at 30 tailoring-variant sets a month while monthly Pro is unlimited.
