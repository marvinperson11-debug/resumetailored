For job seekers

Is the free plan really free?

Yes — no credit card, ever. Free gives you unlimited resume tailoring, cover letters and ATS scans; the LinkedIn optimizer score with all suggestions; Job Decoder (3 per day); all 15 interview questions; the job finder with every result plus job cover letters; unlimited Career Hub goals, roadmap, skill-gap and insights; 6 templates; and 2,000 characters of custom writing instructions. Free exports carry a small watermark. See the full comparison on the pricing page.

What does Pro ($19/month) add?

Pro adds 3 AI tailoring variants per job (conservative, balanced, bold); an ATS rewrite report with keyword delta and before/after score; all 104 templates with watermark-free exports; the Resume Video studio; your own personal website; Deep Job Decoder with job comparison and unlimited decodes; detailed interview feedback and full mock interviews; LinkedIn AI rewrite; and unlimited custom instructions. Details on the pricing page.

What does Pro Lifetime ($129) include?

Everything in Pro for a single one-time payment. The one difference from monthly Pro: Lifetime is capped at 30 tailoring-variant sets per calendar month, while monthly Pro is unlimited. Compare both on the pricing page.

What is the watermark on free exports?

Free exports (PDF, DOCX and TXT) carry a small “Made with ResumeTailored AI” mark in the footer. Pro removes it, along with unlocking all 104 templates. See plans.

Will the AI invent experience I don't have?

No. The AI only rewrites and reframes the experience already on your resume, using the language of the job posting. It never adds skills, employers or achievements you don't have, so everything stays truthful.

How do I cancel?

Anytime. Use the self-serve cancel page or email support@resumetailored.com and we'll handle it within 1 hour. Monthly Pro keeps access through the end of the billing period; Lifetime is a one-time purchase.

Employer Portal

1 active job posting, 10 candidates, 1 seat, 3 e-signature sends per month, 5 documents and a basic career page. See all employer tiers on the pricing page.

What does Portal ($49/month) include?

Unlimited jobs and candidates, 3 seats, 10 e-signature sends per month, 10 video interviews per month with recording, 50 documents, the full career-site builder, plus the Employees hub and Time suite. Compare tiers.

What does Scale ($99/month) include?

Everything in Portal, plus 10 seats, 50 e-signature sends, 50 video interviews with AI summaries, 200 documents and the Office suite. Compare tiers.

What does Corporate ($299/month) include?

Everything in Scale with unlimited everything, plus white-label career pages: your custom domain and no “Powered by” badge. Compare tiers.

What is the Employer Portal?

A hiring workspace for small teams: job posts, an applicant pipeline (New → Reviewed → Interview → Hired), candidate search, interview scheduling, in-app messaging, hiring funnel analytics, e-signatures and a career site — with the Employees hub and Time suite on paid tiers.
