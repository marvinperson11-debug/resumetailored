# Jobscan vs ResumeTailored AI (2026): Scanning vs Rewriting

**Category:** Tool Comparison | **Read time:** 6 min | **Updated:** October 7, 2026

**Source:** https://resumetailored.com/blog/jobscan-vs-resumetailored

---

Jobscan built its name on the resume-to-job-description match report. ResumeTailored AI starts from the same problem but goes one step further and rewrites the resume and cover letter for the posting. Which fits you depends on whether you want a diagnosis or a draft.

> Facts about Jobscan were verified October 7, 2026 against jobscan.co. Its pricing page sits behind a login, so confirm current prices, limits and refund terms on the site before you buy.

## Where Jobscan is stronger

- A dedicated, long-established scanner with a detailed match report and recruiter and ATS findings.
- LinkedIn optimization and a cover-letter scanner on Premium, plus a 7-day trial.
- If a score against one job description is all you need, five free scans a month may be enough.

## Where ResumeTailored AI is stronger

- Tailoring is unlimited on the free plan, and scanning and rewriting happen in one flow.
- A lower paid price ($19.00 versus $49.95 monthly, about $29.98 on Jobscan’s quarterly plan or about $24.95 a month annually).
- Free ATS scanner plus a Pro report showing the keyword changes and a before/after match score.

## How the match score works

Jobscan’s match rate is keyword overlap with the job description, not a simulation of how a real applicant tracking system will rank you. Our own ATS scan is also an estimate. Treat either as a guide to missing keywords, not a verdict.

## What we are not claiming

Jobscan lists AI optimizations and an AI cover letter generator on Premium, so we do not describe it as scan-only. We have not tested which tool’s keyword matching is more accurate. Run one resume and one posting through both and compare.
