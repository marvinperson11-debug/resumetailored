# Build a Professional Resume Website in Minutes with ResumeTailored AI

*By ResumeTailored AI Team · August 5, 2026 · 7 min read*

**Meta description:** Turn your resume into a live personal website in minutes with our free resume website builder — no code, mobile-friendly, and shareable with one clean link.

A PDF resume tells a recruiter what you've done. A **personal website** shows them who you are — the moment they click a link, with no download and no attachment lost in an email thread. ResumeTailored AI's **resume website builder** turns the resume you already have into a live, mobile-friendly site in minutes — free, no code.

## What the resume website builder does

It's a **personal website creator** and a **portfolio website tool** in one: your experience, skills, and summary become sections on a real, indexable page you can send to anyone. Because it's built into ResumeTailored AI, it reuses the resume content you've already tailored — no re-typing.

## How it works, step by step

1. **Start from your resume.** The builder pulls in your saved or tailored resume. New here? See [how to tailor your resume with AI](/blog/how-to-tailor-resume-with-ai).
2. **Pick a template.** Clean, recruiter-friendly layouts that scale to any screen.
3. **Edit inline, in real typography.** Click any element to edit it directly on the page.
4. **Add media a PDF can't hold.** A headshot, project images, or a short [resume video](/tools/resume-video).
5. **Publish and share one link.** Your site goes live at its own address — one link, always current.

## Why a website is worth it for job seekers
- **You stand out instantly** when most applicants send the same PDF.
- **No attachment friction** — a link opens in one tap on any device.
- **You control the narrative** — show projects and context a one-page resume cuts.
- **It's findable** — an indexable site ranks for your name.
- **It's always current** — update once, every shared link reflects it.

## Website, or share-as-link?
**Share-as-link** turns any tailored resume into a private, unlisted page — great for a single application. The **website builder** is the step up: a public, indexable, media-rich personal site for your whole search.
