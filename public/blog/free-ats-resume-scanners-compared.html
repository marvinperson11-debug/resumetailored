Applicant tracking system (ATS) scanners compare your resume with a job description and flag missing keywords and formatting issues. Most have a free tier with limits. Below is what each publishes, with where the information came from. We are one of the four, so read our row with that in mind.

Facts retrieved October 7, 2026 from each company’s own pages (Jobscan’s plan page, Teal’s pricing page and help center, Resume Worded’s tool pages). Free limits change often and some pages disagree with each other; confirm on each site.

ToolFree tierWhat the free tier is best for
Jobscan5 resume scans per month (reported on its plan page)Detailed match reports when you only need a few
TealLimited keyword set (top 5 keywords from a job description), basic resume analysis, unlimited resumes and job trackingOrganising a search and a basic keyword check
Resume WordedFree tools (Score My Resume, Targeted Resume); its pages do not state one clear monthly cap, so check its siteInstant score and line-by-line feedback
ResumeTailored AIUnlimited ATS scans and unlimited tailoring with a free account; Pro adds an ATS rewrite reportScanning and rewriting in one flow

How to choose

  • If you want a score only and apply to a few jobs a month, any free tier may be enough.
  • If you apply to many roles, check the monthly limit first.
  • If you want the rewrite as well as the diagnosis, check whether the tool rewrites on the free plan or only scores.
  • If LinkedIn matters, check which tool includes LinkedIn optimization on its free plan. Our free LinkedIn optimizer gives a score and all suggestions.

A caution about ATS statistics

You will see claims that a large share of resumes are rejected by ATS software before a human sees them. Those figures are widely repeated and poorly sourced. What is well established is simpler: plain structure, standard headings and the posting’s own terms make a resume easier for software and for people to read.
