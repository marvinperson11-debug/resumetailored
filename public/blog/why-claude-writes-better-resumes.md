# Claude vs ChatGPT for Resumes: How to Compare Them Yourself

**Category:** AI Technology | **Read time:** 6 min | **Updated:** October 7, 2026

**Source:** https://resumetailored.com/blog/why-claude-writes-better-resumes

---

ResumeTailored AI is powered by Anthropic’s Claude. An earlier version of this post claimed that we blind-tested Claude against ChatGPT with recruiters and that Claude won. We do not have a published, citable study, so we have removed those claims. Here is what we can honestly say, and how to run your own comparison.

## What we can say

- We chose Claude for resume tailoring because it works well for us on this task. That is a product decision, not a published benchmark.
- Both Claude and ChatGPT can write usable resume content, and results vary by model version and by prompt.
- The biggest quality factor is the input: a specific resume, a specific posting and clear instructions beat any model choice.

## What good AI resume writing looks like

- Specific: names the tools, scale and outcome from your real experience instead of generic phrases.
- True: adds no skills, employers or numbers that are not in your original resume.
- Matched: uses the job posting’s own terms where they apply to you, without stuffing keywords.
- Readable: plain, active sentences a recruiter can scan quickly.

## A fair way to compare models yourself

- Pick one resume and one job posting.
- Give each tool the same inputs and ask for the same outputs (a tailored summary, three bullets and a cover letter).
- Score each output against the four qualities above, ideally with a friend who does not know which is which.
- Check every claim against your real history. Any invented detail is a reason to reject the output.

## Guardrails matter more than the model

Whatever model you use, review the result. AI tools can introduce details that were not in your resume. You are the one who has to explain it in an interview, so edit anything you cannot stand behind.
