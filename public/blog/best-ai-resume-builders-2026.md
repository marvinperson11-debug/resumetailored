# The Best AI Resume Builders in 2026 (Honest Comparison)

An honest comparison of the top AI resume tools — what each does best, what it costs, and who it's for.

## How we think about "best"
"Best" depends on the job you're hiring the tool for: building once, tracking applications, scoring keywords, or tailoring to each posting.

## The 2026 shortlist

### 1. ResumeTailored AI
**Best for tailoring to a specific job** — $19.00/mo · free tier

Built around one job: rewriting your existing resume to match each job description using Anthropic's Claude, then writing a matching cover letter. Includes a job-URL importer, 104 ATS-ready templates, PDF/Word export and a bilingual English/Chinese interface. Unlimited tailoring, free, no card.

### 2. Teal
**Best free job-tracker** — free plan; Teal+ about $29 per 30 days

Strong free job-application tracker. Its free plan includes a limited number of AI credits (including a few for cover letters); unlimited AI generation is part of the paid Teal+ plan.

### 3. Jobscan
**Best keyword scorer** — $49.95/mo, or $89.95 per quarter (about $30/mo)

Excellent at scoring how well your resume matches a job description and flagging missing keywords. Its Premium plan also lists AI optimizations and an AI cover letter generator; check Jobscan's site for current features.

### 4. Rezi
**Solid ATS-focused builder** — $29/mo or ~$149 lifetime

A capable ATS-oriented resume builder with AI writing features. Its free plan lists unlimited cover letters; we have not verified which AI model it uses.

### 5. Kickresume
**Best template variety** — from $8/mo billed yearly ($24 monthly)

Large library of designed templates and a low entry price. Some two-column template designs can reduce ATS parse reliability, so test exports against a scanner.

### 6. Enhancv
**Best for visual design** — free 7-day plan; Pro quarterly (see Enhancv's site)

Design-forward templates and content tips. Great if polished visuals are your priority. Enhancv's pricing page also lists an ATS check and resume tailoring to job descriptions, so compare it with the others on your own resume.

## Why tailoring beats building
Keyword-based ATS screening rewards a resume that matches each posting. ResumeTailored focuses on per-job tailoring with Anthropic's Claude — a rewritten, ATS-aligned resume plus a matching cover letter in ~30 seconds, keeping every claim truthful.
