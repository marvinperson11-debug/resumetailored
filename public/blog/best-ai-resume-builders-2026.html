How we think about "best"

"Best" depends on the job you're hiring the tool for. Building a resume once, tracking applications, scoring keywords, and tailoring to each posting are different problems. Below, each tool is rated for what it genuinely does well — including where competitors beat us.

How we checked prices

We opened each tool's pricing page on November 19, 2026 and wrote down the free tier limits and starting prices exactly as listed. Where a pricing page did not render its table, we used the vendor's own FAQ and recent independent reads of the same page. Prices change without notice, so confirm at checkout. We did not run a hands-on test of every tool; this is a pricing and free-tier comparison, not a review of output quality.

The 2026 shortlist

1. ResumeTailored AI

Best for tailoring to a specific job

$19/mo or $129 lifetime · free tier

Built around one job: rewriting your existing resume to match each job description using Anthropic's Claude, then writing a matching cover letter. Includes a job-URL importer, 104 ATS-ready templates, PDF/Word export and a bilingual English/Chinese interface. Unlimited tailoring, free, no card.

2. Teal

Best free job-tracker

Free plan · Teal+ $13/week, $29 per 30 days, $79 per 90 days

Strong free job-application tracker and Chrome extension. Its free plan includes a limited number of AI credits (5 resume-bullet, 2 summary, 2 cover-letter credits); unlimited AI generation is part of the paid Teal+ plan.

3. Jobscan

Best keyword scorer

$49.95/mo, or $89.95 per quarter (about $30/mo)

Excellent at scoring how well your resume matches a job description and flagging missing keywords. Its Premium plan also lists AI optimizations and an AI cover letter generator; check Jobscan's site for current features. Free tier covers 5 match scans a month.

4. Rezi

Solid ATS-focused builder

$29/mo or $149 lifetime

A capable ATS-oriented resume builder with AI writing features. Its free plan covers 1 resume, 1 AI interview, and 3 PDF downloads, with unlimited cover letters; we have not verified which AI model it uses.

5. Kickresume

Best template variety

from $8/mo billed yearly ($24 monthly)

Large library of designed templates and a low entry price. Free covers 4 templates with unlimited downloads, but no AI Writer. Some two-column template designs can reduce ATS parse reliability, so test exports against a scanner.

6. Enhancv

Best for visual design

Free 7 days only · Pro $39/mo, $69 per 3 months, $99 per 6 months

Design-forward templates and content tips. Great if polished visuals are your priority. The free plan lasts 7 days only, and Pro prices are list prices — Enhancv runs rotating discounts, so the checkout price may be lower. Enhancv’s pricing page also lists an ATS check and resume tailoring to job descriptions, so compare it with the others on your own resume.

7. Resume.io

Best for a quick one-off resume

Free · $2.95 7-day trial, then $29.95 every 4 weeks

Simple builder with clean templates. The free tier only downloads in TXT format, so a real application needs the trial or a paid plan. Watch the renewal: $29.95 every 4 weeks, not monthly. Quarterly is $49.95.

8. Huntr

Best for tailoring plus tracking

Free · Pro $40/mo, $90 per 3 months, $160 per 6 months

The direct rival for tailoring: unlimited AI tailoring, cover letters, and resume reviews on Pro, plus job tracking in the same place. Free covers 2 tailored resumes and 100 tracked jobs. Pro is the priciest here at $40 a month.

Why tailoring beats building

Most builders help you format one resume. But keyword-based ATS screening rewards a resume that matches each posting. That's why ResumeTailored focuses on per-job tailoring with Anthropic's Claude: paste your resume and a job posting, and get a rewritten, ATS-aligned resume plus a matching cover letter in about 30 seconds — while keeping every claim truthful.

Frequently asked questions

What's the best AI resume builder in 2026?
It depends on your goal. For tailoring an existing resume to a specific job description, ResumeTailored is purpose-built. For job tracking, Teal is strong; for keyword scoring, Jobscan.
Do AI resume builders fabricate experience?
Good ones don't. ResumeTailored only reframes and rewrites your real experience in the job's language — it never invents skills or roles. Always review any AI output for accuracy.
Are any of these free?
Several have free tiers with limits. ResumeTailored gives unlimited tailoring (resume + cover letter) free, with no credit card.
Which AI resume builder is cheapest?
ResumeTailored, Teal, Jobscan, Rezi, Kickresume, Resume.io, and Huntr all have free tiers, but the limits differ a lot. ResumeTailored's free tier covers unlimited tailoring; Teal's covers unlimited job tracking with capped AI credits. On paid plans, Kickresume is cheapest at $8 a month billed yearly, and Huntr's Pro is the priciest at $40 a month.
