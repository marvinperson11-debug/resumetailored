Most hiring tools are built for enterprises with a full talent-acquisition team and a five-figure budget. If you're a small business owner, an office manager, or a founder wearing the recruiter hat, that leaves you stitching together a spreadsheet, an inbox, and a calendar to run every search. The **ResumeTailored AI Employer Portal** replaces that patchwork with a single **recruiter dashboard** — a lightweight **applicant tracking system** (ATS) designed for small teams.

You can [open the Employer Portal](/employer) now — it's free to start.

## What is the Employer Portal?

The Employer Portal is a self-serve **hiring platform** built into ResumeTailored AI, completely separate from the job-seeker side: a dedicated recruiter dashboard with its own interface, its own navigation, and none of the resume-tailoring tabs a candidate would use. Sign in with the same account, flip on "Hire Mode" by adding your company name, and you're in a workspace built for one job: filling roles.

Under the hood it's a true **candidate management software** stack — job postings, an applicant pipeline, interview scheduling, messaging, and analytics — but the surface is deliberately simple. No implementation project, no per-seat sales call.

## Key features

### A dashboard that answers "where do things stand?"
Four numbers that matter: **active job postings**, **applications this week**, **candidates in pipeline**, and **interviews scheduled** — plus a recent-activity feed and quick actions.

### Job posting and management
Post a role with title, description, requirements, location, salary range, job type, and deadline. Every posting lands in a clean table (title, location, posted date, applications, status). A "Need Staff Fast" gig mode covers temp and same-day work.

### Candidate management and a real hiring pipeline
Move applicants through **New → Reviewed → Interview → Hired** (or Rejected, with an optional polite template email). Rate candidates, leave team notes, search an opt-in candidate database, and bookmark strong candidates for future roles.

### Interviews and messaging in one place
Schedule video, phone, or onsite interviews — the portal emails the candidate an invite. In-app messaging keeps every conversation in one thread.

### Analytics and reporting
A **hiring funnel**, average **time-to-hire**, applications-by-source, and one-click CSV export.

## Who it's for
- Small business owners making their first hires
- Startup founders who are the whole hiring team
- Agencies and staffing coordinators
- Office and operations managers hiring hourly, seasonal, or gig roles

## How it saves time
The Employer Portal collapses the job board, inbox, spreadsheet, and calendar into one system, so a resume that arrives Monday is one click from "Reviewed" and two from an interview invite. Because thousands of job seekers already [tailor their resumes with AI](/blog/how-to-tailor-resume-with-ai) on ResumeTailored, applicants tend to arrive with cleaner, better-matched resumes.
