> Rezi information is from rezi.ai/pricing, checked October 7, 2026.

## What each tool offers

- Rezi: unlimited cover letters on the free plan and on paid plans (per Rezi’s FAQ and plan comparison).
- ResumeTailored AI: a cover letter is generated alongside the tailored resume from the same job description, unlimited with a free account.

## Five checks for any AI cover letter

- Is the first sentence about this job and company, or could it open any letter? Replace generic openers.
- Does it name two or three requirements from the posting and answer each with a real example from your experience?
- Are the facts yours? Remove any claim, number or skill you cannot defend in an interview.
- Is it under one page, with a clear close and a reason you want this role?
- Does it sound like you? Read it aloud and cut anything you would never say.

## How ResumeTailored AI approaches it

The letter is generated from your resume and the job posting together, so it can refer to the posting’s own requirements. It is a draft: you should still read it against the checklist above and edit it. We keep every claim tied to what is in your resume.

## The fair test

If you are deciding between tools, paste the same resume and posting into each tool’s free plan and score the letters with the checklist. That tells you more than any vendor comparison, including this one.
