Frequently asked questions

What happens to candidate data in the portal?
Applicant resumes and messages live inside your employer workspace. There's no self-serve bulk export or deletion tool for candidate/applicant data yet — that's a gap we're honest about today. To request an export or deletion of candidate data, email support@resumetailored.com.
What can my teammates do once they're added?
Free includes 1 seat for one non-owner employee — the account owner doesn't count. Portal adds 3 team seats, Scale adds 10, and Corporate is unlimited. Every teammate added to the workspace shares the same access — there isn't a granular per-role permission system yet.
Can I export my data?
Not for candidates yet — there's no bulk CSV export of applicant data today. Portal and up can export employee timesheets to CSV under the Time suite, which is a separate payroll feature.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly employer plans cancel anytime from your account.
How do I get support?
Email support@resumetailored.com — the same address listed on our About page.
