How to raise your score

  1. Mirror the posting's own words. If the job description says "project management" and your resume says "managed projects," use the exact phrase from the posting too.
  2. Add missing skills you genuinely have. If you are missing a word because you described it differently, add the exact term, but only if it is true.
  3. Keep every claim true. Never add a skill or experience you do not actually have, just to raise a score.
  4. Re-check. After you update your resume, paste the new version back in and see how your score changes.
